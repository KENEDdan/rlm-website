Staff security sheet

Rebuilding Lives Ministry website · keep this at your desk · the people who trust us with prayer requests deserve our care

DO Your account

  • Use a long passphrase (12+ characters) that you use nowhere else.
  • Keep the authenticator app on your own phone with a screen lock.
  • Store your 10 backup codes on paper in a safe place.
  • Lock your screen every time you step away (Windows + L).
  • Sign out on shared or public computers.
  • Keep your computer and phone updated.

DON'T Ever

  • Share your password, codes or account, not even with the Founder.
  • Write your password on a sticky note or in a chat.
  • Save prayer requests or volunteer lists on personal or shared devices.
  • Forward submissions by email or WhatsApp.
  • Type bank details anywhere except the admin (Finance only).
  • Sign in from an internet café.

DO Personal data

  • Read submissions only inside the admin; it is encrypted and logged.
  • Respect "keep confidential" prayer requests.
  • Export to CSV only when truly needed, then delete the file.
  • Publish photos and testimonies only with consent; extra care with children.

DON'T Fall for phishing

  • The website never asks for your code or password by email, SMS or phone.
  • Be suspicious of urgency: "account suspended", "sign in now", "urgent transfer".
  • Check the address bar before typing your password.
  • "New bank details" emails are a classic fraud: phone the bank.

Something looks wrong?

Tell the Founder immediately by phone. Lost phone, a sign-in you did not make, changes nobody made, a suspicious email you clicked: reporting quickly is never a mistake. Do not delete anything.
