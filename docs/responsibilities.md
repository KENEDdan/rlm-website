REBUILDING LIVES MINISTRY (RLM)

Major Responsibilities of Leadership Positions

The following are the proposed major responsibilities of each departmental leader within Rebuilding Lives Ministry. Each leader is responsible for planning, coordinating, supervising, implementing, and reporting on activities within their department in alignment with the ministry’s vision, mission, values, and strategic objectives.

1. Membership Administration

Primary responsibility: Managing membership records, recruitment, communication, and general administrative coordination of ministry members and volunteers.

Major responsibilities:

* Maintain an updated register and database of all ministry members and volunteers.

* Coordinate membership registration, admission, orientation, and integration.

* Maintain personal information, contact details, and service records of members.

* Monitor members’ participation, commitment, and involvement in ministry activities.

* Coordinate internal communication, announcements, meetings, and official correspondence.

* Maintain departmental records, minutes, reports, and administrative documents.

* Coordinate membership welfare and address administrative concerns.

* Prepare periodic membership reports for ministry leadership.

2. Finance and Resource Mobilization Officer

Primary responsibility: To oversee the ministry’s financial management, accountability, fundraising, donor relations, and resource mobilization to ensure financial sustainability and effective implementation of ministry activities.

Major responsibilities

* Prepare and manage the ministry’s annual budget.

* Record and monitor all ministry income, donations, expenditure, and financial transactions.

* Identify and approach potential donors, sponsors, churches, and funding organizations.

* Develop fundraising strategies and coordinate resource mobilization initiatives.

* Prepare donor appeal letters, funding proposals, and partnership requests.

* Maintain relationships with existing donors and financial partners.

* Identify grant opportunities and coordinate funding applications.

* Maintain accurate financial records, receipts, vouchers, and supporting documents.

* Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports.

* Monitor departmental budgets and ensure responsible use of ministry resources.

* Provide financial updates and accountability reports to donors and ministry leadership.

* Develop strategies for long-term financial sustainability.

3. Logistics and Procurement

Primary responsibility: Ensuring that the ministry has the necessary materials, equipment, transportation, supplies, and logistical arrangements to implement its activities effectively.

Major responsibilities:

* Identify and coordinate the procurement of materials and equipment required by the ministry.

* Obtain quotations, compare prices, and recommend reliable suppliers.

* Coordinate purchasing, delivery, storage, and distribution of ministry supplies.

* Organize transportation, accommodation, feeding, and other logistical arrangements for ministry events.

* Maintain an inventory of ministry assets, equipment, and supplies.

* Ensure proper maintenance and responsible use of ministry property.

* Coordinate the logistical preparation of conferences, crusades, fellowships, and outreach programs.

* Maintain procurement records, delivery notes, and inventory reports.

* Ensure transparency and cost-effectiveness in all procurement activities.

4. Media and Communications

Primary responsibility: Managing the ministry’s public image, digital communication, media production, documentation, and visibility.

Major responsibilities:

* Manage the ministry’s website, social media platforms, and other digital communication channels.

* Design posters, banners, flyers, announcements, and promotional materials.

* Photograph, record, and document ministry programs and activities.

* Produce and edit videos, photographs, testimonies, interviews, and ministry reports.

* Publicize upcoming conferences, evangelism programs, fellowships, and other activities.

* Develop and maintain the ministry’s media archives and digital records.

* Communicate ministry achievements, testimonies, and impact to partners and supporters.

* Ensure consistency in the ministry’s branding, messaging, and public communication.

* Support online discipleship, teaching, worship, and evangelism through digital platforms.

5. Missions and Outreach

Primary responsibility: Coordinating evangelism, missionary activities, community engagement, and the expansion of the Gospel to unreached communities.

Major responsibilities:

* Plan and coordinate evangelism campaigns, open-air crusades, and missionary outreaches.

* Identify communities, schools, institutions, and geographical areas requiring Gospel outreach.

* Mobilize and coordinate evangelism teams and mission volunteers.

* Organize hospital, prison, community, and institutional visitations where appropriate.

* Coordinate follow-up activities for new converts in collaboration with the Discipleship Department.

* Establish relationships with local churches, pastors, and community leaders.

* Identify opportunities for ministry expansion into new communities and geographical locations.

* Coordinate mission trips, evangelism materials, and field activities.

* Prepare mission reports documenting souls reached, decisions for Christ, challenges, and testimonies.

6. Discipleship Department

Primary responsibility: Nurturing spiritual growth, biblical understanding, Christian maturity, and leadership development among new converts, believers, and ministry members.

Major responsibilities:

* Develop and coordinate structured discipleship programs and Bible study materials.

* Organize discipleship classes, spiritual growth sessions, and Bible study fellowships.

* Establish follow-up systems for new converts and individuals who respond to the Gospel.

* Train and equip believers in prayer, evangelism, biblical knowledge, and Christian living.

* Identify, mentor, and develop emerging Christian leaders.

* Organize spiritual mentorship and accountability programs.

* Coordinate regular spiritual growth assessments and discipleship progress reports.

* Encourage members to develop consistent personal prayer, Bible study, and devotional lives.

* Work with the Missions and Outreach Department to ensure that new converts are properly discipled and integrated into Christian fellowship.

7. Development and Infrastructure

Primary responsibility: Overseeing the physical development, infrastructure, facilities, long-term institutional growth, and sustainability of the ministry.

Major responsibilities:

* Develop and implement infrastructure and institutional development plans.

* Coordinate the acquisition, development, and management of ministry properties and facilities.

* Oversee construction, renovation, and maintenance of ministry buildings.

* Develop proposals for ministry infrastructure projects and capital development.

* Identify potential development partners, contractors, and technical professionals.

* Coordinate feasibility assessments, architectural designs, engineering works, and construction supervision through qualified professionals.

* Develop plans for ministry facilities such as offices, conference halls, hostels, training centres, and worship facilities.

* Monitor infrastructure project budgets, timelines, quality, and implementation progress.

* Identify long-term development opportunities that strengthen the ministry’s operational capacity and sustainability.

* Prepare periodic infrastructure and development reports for ministry leadership.
